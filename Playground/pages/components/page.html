Page Component

The content layout engine — readable max-width by default, breakout for full-width sections, and .maxwidth-container for full-bleed highlight bands.

1. Default: Constrained to Readable Width

<nui-page> is the container the router creates automatically for every loaded fragment. By default, every direct child is constrained to --space-page-maxwidth (~56rem) and receives consistent gutter padding (var(--nui-space) var(--nui-space-double)). Constrained children are left-aligned.

Constrained Section — capped at --space-page-maxwidth (~56rem) with 32px side gutter.

2. Breakout: Full Width with Text Gutter

Adding the breakout attribute removes the max-width constraint so the element expands to 100% of the container. Crucially, it keeps the exact same horizontal padding as standard sections, so its content mirrors the text-flow gutter instead of slamming against the viewport edge. Ideal for wide tables, data grids, code editors, and responsive layouts.

Breakout Content — spans the full container width (100%), but text starts at the exact same left gutter (32px) as the constrained section above.

3. Full-Bleed Highlight Band (.maxwidth-container)

When you want a section to have a different background (a visual highlight, colored band, or shaded banner) that spans edge-to-edge across the entire viewport, but keep its text inside the readable column:

  1. Add breakout to the <section> so its background bleeds across the entire container width.
  2. Wrap the inner content in <div class="maxwidth-container">.

The background bleeds 100% edge-to-edge with no horizontal gap, while the text inside .maxwidth-container aligns pixel-perfectly with standard sections above and below.

Highlight Band Content — background spans 100% edge-to-edge, text is constrained to --space-page-maxwidth and perfectly aligned.

The Contract at a Glance

The layout contract between the router, the theme, and page fragments:

  • nui-content fills the viewport minus space-occupying sidebars.
  • nui-main fills nui-content and is the vertical scroll container.
  • nui-page is the unconstrained inner wrapper of the scroll area.
  • Standard children get max-width constraint + gutter padding + vertical rhythm.
  • [breakout] children expand to 100% width, keeping gutter padding.
  • [breakout]:has(.maxwidth-container) removes outer horizontal padding so the background bleeds edge-to-edge, while .maxwidth-container provides the constraint and gutter.
  • Fragment rule: Top-level sections must be direct children of nui-page. Do not wrap page fragments in an outer layout container div.
