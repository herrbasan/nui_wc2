AI Atom — Deck Fixture

Step 1 of the teaching series, rendered by nui-slides — a real deck from another project, copied in to test the renderer against prose it was not written for.

The fixture

Source: storage/teaching/AI_Atom/slides/01-the-floor.md. The copy is verbatim except for image links: the eight illustrations were downscaled to 1600 px WebP (~17 MB of PNG became ~750 KB) and .png was rewritten to .webp in the markdown.

What to watch: preset=image inside two columns, preset=image:hero paired with preset=card:scrim:bottom (anchored scrim overlay over the hero diagram), and a narration block on every slide.

Renderer result. 8/8 slides mount with zero overflow, all eight images load, every slide has narration, and the blocks editor round-trips the file without loss.

Mode. The deck is forced to mode="light": its artwork is drawn on light paper and must not invert with a dark host.

Overlay via preset variant (Layer 2). Slide 6 pairs preset=image:hero with preset=card:scrim:bottom. In page view (nui-markdown) the unknown 3rd segment degrades gracefully to an ordinary scrim card below the diagram; in nui-slides it anchors over the bottom edge of the canvas with zero JSON and zero block IDs.
