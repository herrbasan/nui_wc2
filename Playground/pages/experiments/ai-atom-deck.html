AI Atom — Deck Fixture

Step 1 of the teaching series, rendered by nui-slides — a real deck from another project, copied in to test the renderer against prose it was not written for.

The fixture

Source: storage/teaching/AI_Atom/slides/01-the-floor.md. The copy is verbatim except for image links: the eight illustrations were downscaled to 1600 px WebP (~17 MB of PNG became ~750 KB) and .png was rewritten to .webp in the markdown.

What to watch: preset=image inside two columns, preset=image:hero paired with card overlay=bottom (an attribute this renderer does not model), and a narration block on every slide.

Renderer result. 8/8 slides mount, all eight images load, every slide has narration, and the blocks editor round-trips the file without loss — including overlay=bottom, which it preserves even though it does not model it.

Mode. The deck is forced to mode="light": its artwork is drawn on light paper and must not invert with a dark host.

Known gap. The spec does not define overlay, so the card on slide 6 stacks under the hero image instead of covering its bottom edge. The slide still fits — media takes the height the text leaves — but the composition is not the one the deck asked for. Either the token becomes vocabulary or the deck must stop relying on it.
