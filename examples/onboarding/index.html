How billing works

The workspace runs on the free tier until you exceed the included usage. After that, the budget you set becomes a monthly cap rather than a fixed charge.

Can I change the region later?

Not directly — data residency is fixed once a workspace holds production data. A new workspace can be created in another region and migrated into.

Who can see the invited team?

Only members of the workspace. A pending invite can be withdrawn at any time.
