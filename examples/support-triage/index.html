Escalation policy

Billing disputes

Route to the billing queue and attach the last invoice. Do not offer a credit before finance has confirmed the charge.

Data loss

Escalate immediately, regardless of queue load. A confirmed loss opens an incident and the customer is notified by the incident lead.

Feature requests

Close as a duplicate when the request exists in the roadmap. Otherwise tag it and let the product team pick it up weekly.
