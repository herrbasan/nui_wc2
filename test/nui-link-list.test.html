nui-link-list — fold-mode depth indentation

Fold mode removes the nested-ul padding that gives tree mode its depth, so depth has to be stated. These checks assert what the reader sees: a child's icon sits in the indent rather than at the row's left edge, a sub-group indents past its parent, and a deeper leaf indents further (issue #62).
